**Retail Sales Sample Dashboard**



I built this Power BI dashboard using a 13,000-row retail sales sample. I removed cancellations and rows with nonpositive quantity or unit price, leaving 12,734 rows for analysis.



The dashboard shows sample revenue, revenue by month, and revenue by country. Revenue is calculated as quantity × unit price.



The figures describe this sample and should not be treated as totals for the full retail dataset.
